Description

Madden Property Consultants are delighted to present this bright and well-presented two-bedroom apartment in the highly sought-after Castleforbes Square development, in the north Docklands.

Situated on the fourth floor, this attractive apartment enjoys a west-facing aspect and benefits from a large balcony, providing an excellent outdoor space and plenty of natural light throughout the afternoon and evening.

The accommodation comprises a spacious open-plan living, kitchen and dining area, creating a contemporary and versatile space ideal for both everyday living and entertaining. The kitchen has been renovated to an exceptional level. There are two well-proportioned bedrooms, both facing onto an internal courtyard and a large family bathroom.

The property has also benefited from an upgraded heating system and achieves a B3 BER rating, offering a good balance of comfort and energy efficiency.

A further significant benefit is the provision of designated parking, adding convenience and peace of mind for residents.

Overall, this is a well-appointed apartment offering bright, modern accommodation, generous outdoor space and secure parking in a popular Dublin development. The property would appeal to first-time buyers, owner-occupiers and investors alike.
